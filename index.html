BACKGROUND & PHILOSOPHY

Curiosity Meets Connected Technology

Bridging physical sensors with practical problem-solving.

I'm Varish Shukla, pursuing a B.Tech in Internet of Things (IoT). I'm interested in exploring how connected technologies can help address practical, real-world challenges. My project portfolio highlights concepts around smart plant watering and water leakage detection.

As an undergraduate student and aspiring IoT developer, my work revolves around hands-on experimentation: understanding sensor interfaces, microcontroller architectures, logic thresholds, and reliable notification triggers.

I am eager to apply this curiosity to client projects, academic collaborations, and practical prototyping needs where connected hardware can automate tasks and protect resources.
